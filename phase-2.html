Why this phase exists

Security before autonomy.

Putting native AI into the image before process isolation, capability enforcement and key lifecycle would reproduce the same ambient-authority problem ORYVAEL is designed to eliminate.

01

Deny by default

Missing authority is a denial. Privilege is never inferred from intelligence, identity names or convenience.

02

No self-grant

A principal cannot widen its own authority. AI may request capability; it cannot manufacture capability.

03

Explicit principals

Humans, services, applications, devices and AI workers are distinct identities with distinct authority.

04

Attribution everywhere

Privileged actions must identify the principal, process, session, object, capability and policy decision.

Independent failure domains

Each process receives its own address space, kernel execution context and handle table. A page fault or invalid instruction in one userspace process must terminate or isolate that process — not halt the machine.

  • saved CPU contexts and real context switching
  • multiple runnable and blocked ring-3 processes
  • per-process address spaces
  • recoverable process faults
  • explicit create / start / exit / kill lifecycle

2.4 — 2.5

Users are principals.
Sandboxes are native.

ORYVAEL does not need to make Unix superuser semantics its foundation. POSIX compatibility can exist later without defining the native trust model.

Authenticated sessions

A login resolves to a human principal and session object carrying method, credential identity, source, expiry and capabilities.

Attributed audit

Every privileged transition records who acted, which object was targeted, which capability applied and what decision occurred.

2.9 — 2.10

The AI is native.
It is not sovereign.

The model never belongs in the kernel and never becomes an authorization oracle. AI workers execute in ring 3 as ordinary principals and reach the system through capability-enforced APIs.

Definition of done

Phase 2 ends with evidence.

The milestone is complete only when the complete authority chain is demonstrated in one bootable native image.

  1. 01

    Multiple independent ring-3 processes are context-switched by the native scheduler.

  2. 02

    A userspace fault kills or isolates one process without terminating unrelated workloads.

  3. 03

    Kernel objects are accessed only through kernel-managed handles and rights.

  4. 04

    Absent, expired and revoked capabilities fail closed.

  5. 05

    Authenticated sessions resolve to explicit human principals.

  6. 06

    SSH and machine private keys are generated or provisioned outside source code.

  7. 07

    Privileged operations emit attributed, tamper-evident audit events.

  8. 08

    A native AI worker runs as a restricted ring-3 principal.

  9. 09

    The AI detects or analyzes a condition but cannot self-grant the requested response action.

  10. 10

    A scoped temporary capability is authorized, exercised and revoked with preserved evidence.

Result

ORYVAEL becomes a governed application and intelligence substrate.
